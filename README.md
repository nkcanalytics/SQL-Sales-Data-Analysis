# 📊 SQL Sales Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales and customer data using SQL. It includes 16 practical SQL queries designed to solve real-world business problems related to sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product categories, and revenue analysis.

The project demonstrates practical SQL skills and the ability to convert business questions into meaningful database queries.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
- Calculate revenue across product categories.
- Identify the highest-revenue-generating city.
- Find top-spending customers.
- Analyze order status and order frequency.
- Identify popular product categories.
- Evaluate customer purchase quantities.

## 🗂️ Database Tables

The project uses two tables:

### 1. Sales
Contains sales and order-related information:
- Customer Name
- City
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price
- Order Status

### 2. Customer1
Contains customer information:
- Customer Name
- Email
- Phone
- City

## 🔍 SQL Business Questions (16 Queries)

### Basic SQL Analysis
1. Display customer name, city, product category, quantity, and price from the Sales table.
2. Display all orders where order status is Delivered.
3. Display customer name and price in descending order of price.

### Revenue and Aggregate Analysis
4. Find the total revenue of each product category.
5. Find the number of orders placed by customers.
6. Find the average price of each product category.
7. Display the top 3 highest-priced orders.
8. Display customers whose order status is Returned.

### SQL Joins and Business Analysis
9. Join Sales and Customer1 tables to display customer name, email, phone, city, product category, and price.
10. Identify which city generated the highest revenue.
11. Identify the customer who spent the most money.
12. Identify the product category with the highest number of orders.

### Customer Purchase Analysis
13. Display customers who have placed more than one order.
14. Display customers who purchased more than 5 total items.
15. Display product categories where total revenue is greater than 7000.
16. Identify the customer who purchased the highest total quantity.
